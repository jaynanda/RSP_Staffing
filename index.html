About RSP Staffing

Built on Organic Growth and Upward Momentum.

Headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming and serving employers and professionals nationwide across the United States, RSP Staffing (Recruitment Staffing Professional) bridges the gap between ambitious organizations and accomplished talent.

Our emblem tells our story: the living tree represents deep roots, ethical relationships, and foundational stability, while the upward arrow reflects forward career mobility and measurable business acceleration.

Our Mission

To empower U.S. enterprises with dependable, domain-vetted professionals while providing candidates with transparent advocacy and career-defining projects.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted human-centered recruitment partner for technology, healthcare, finance, and manufacturing teams across the United States.

Industries We Serve

Healthcare

Supporting hospitals, health systems, and digital health innovators with clinical support talent, healthcare IT specialists, EHR analysts, and revenue cycle professionals.

Banking and Financial Services

Finance and Accounting

Manufacturing
